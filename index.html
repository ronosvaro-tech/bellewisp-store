FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it hard the first time?

There's a small learning curve — expect your first set to take a bit longer. Start with fewer clusters on the outer corners, then build in. Many first-time buyers in the reviews say it was easier than they expected.

How long do they stay on?

It depends on your lashes and routine. Buyers commonly report wearing a set for several days, including through showers and sleep, when the seal is applied. Avoid oil-based products near your lashes while wearing them.

How do I remove them?

Use an oil-based lash or makeup remover, let it soften the bond for a minute, then slide the clusters off gently. Never pull them off dry — that can take natural lashes with them.

Are they safe for sensitive eyes?

The bond is applied to your lashes, not your skin or eye. If you have sensitive eyes or allergies, do a patch test 24 hours before your first use and stop using it if you notice irritation. Not for use on children.

Which style should I choose?

Not sure? Go with Natural (30D) — it's the most popular choice by far among buyers of this kit. Want to try more than one? The 3-kit bundle lets you mix styles.
