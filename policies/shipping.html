Shipping Policy

Processing

Each kit is packed in our signature Bellewisp box. Orders are processed and dispatched within 5⁠–⁠10 business days.

Delivery

Free tracked shipping to the United States. Delivery typically takes 5⁠–⁠10 business days after dispatch. You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for 10 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days, email us and we'll reship or refund.
