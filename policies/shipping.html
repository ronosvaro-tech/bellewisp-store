Shipping Policy

Processing

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days.

Delivery

Free tracked shipping to the United States. Typical delivery time is 7–14 business days after dispatch. You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States only.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for 10 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days, email us and we'll reship or refund.
