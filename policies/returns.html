Returns & Refunds

30-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with your kit for any reason, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll issue a full refund of the product price.

How to request a refund

  • Email support@bellewisp.com with your order email and a short note.
  • For hygiene reasons we don't require used lash products to be sent back.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method (PayPal / card) within 5 business days of approval.

Damaged or wrong item

Send us a photo within 30 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or refund — your choice.
